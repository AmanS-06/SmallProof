How a question is answered

Small CPU models and rules do the deciding. The SLM only writes the final answer from five passages, and code checks it before anyone sees it.

1Understand

Company and year are matched exactly against the corpus vocabulary; a zero-shot classifier picks the statement section. Tags boost, they never filter.

2Search

BM25 and dense search (bge-small in Chroma), plus a search inside the tagged filing, merged with reciprocal rank fusion.

3Rerank

A MiniLM cross-encoder orders 30 candidates. If the question needs a balance sheet, income or cash flow statement, that page gets a reserved slot.

4Write

Qwen3 4B (Ollama, GPU) answers from the top 5 passages with page citations, or says NOT FOUND.

5Verify

Code redoes every written calculation and corrects slips. Every number must be in the passages, in the question, or the result of a checked step. A value must come from the column of the year the sentence names. Hedged or cut-off answers are refused.

Why verify in code

Before the check, the 4B model answered 23 of 100 test questions wrongly (each answer reviewed by hand). Many were arithmetic slips, numbers with no source, values from the wrong year column, or answers that hedged and ended in "NOT FOUND". The model also passed such answers when asked to grade itself. Code can redo arithmetic exactly.

What the colours mean

Limits

The check catches numbers with no source, arithmetic slips and wrong year columns. It cannot catch a correct number used for the wrong line item, or a wrong yes/no judgement in prose. Those still need a stronger reader or a human.
